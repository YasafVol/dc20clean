# DC20 Overview Video

I made a quick overview of DC20's rules that I go over in <https://youtu.be/298EA6woVfo>.

*Beta Note: The video is somewhat "out of date" at this point, but I will be making another one soon!*

I made this video to explain DC20 quickly to someone who has never heard of it before. This is not only for YOU (the person reading this right now) but also for your friends that you might want to play the game with. I go over the big and small rules, and after watching, people should have a good idea if they want to try it out or not. I hope this helps those "Session Zeros" of DC20, and I will continue to make more guides and tools like this on my YouTube Channel as well.
