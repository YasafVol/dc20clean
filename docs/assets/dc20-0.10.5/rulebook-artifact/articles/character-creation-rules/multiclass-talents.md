# Multiclass Talents

Multiclass Talents allow you to gain Features from other Classes, except Flavor Features (see Class Flavor Features below). Some Multiclass Talents also allow you to gain Subclass Features, which can be from any Class (including your own). You can't gain the same Feature more than once.

*Class Flavor Features*: Once you gain 2 Class Features from the same Class, you automatically gain that Class's Flavor Feature.

**Example:** *If a Wizard gains 2 Barbarian Features, they automatically gain the Barbarian's Flavor Feature: Shattering Force.*

*Specific Damage Types*: When you gain a Feature that references a damage type of a different Feature, you use that same damage type. If there are multiple choices of damage types to choose from, you must choose one when you gain the Feature.

**Example:** *The Cleric Feature:* **Channel Divinity** *has an option (Censure Divine) that deals "Divine Damage" to nearby creatures of your choice. The Cleric normally choose their type of Divine Damage at level 1 when they gain the* **Cleric Order** *Feature. If a Champion uses a Multiclassing Talent to gain the* **Channel Divinity** *Feature, they would need to choose a type of Divine Damage listed in the* **Cleric Order** *Feature.*

## Novice Multiclass

*Multiclass Talent*

*You can take this Talent multiple times*

You gain a Level 1 Class Feature from any Class.

## Adept Multiclass

*Multiclass Talent*

*Requirement: Level 4*

*You can take this Talent multiple times*

You gain the Level 2 Class Feature of any Class.

## Expert Multiclass

*Multiclass Talent*

*Requirement: Level 6*

*You can take this Talent multiple times*

You gain 1 of the following from a Class that you have at least 1 Class Feature from:

- Its Level 5 Class Expert Feature.
- The Level 3 Subclass Feature and Flavor Feature of one of its Subclasses.

*DC Tip: You may not gain the full benefit of a Level 3 or 5 Feature gained in this way if you don't have the Features that it upgrades.*

*Multiclass Talent*

*Requirement: Level 8*

*You can take this Talent multiple times*

You gain the Level 7 Subclass Expert Feature of a Subclass you have the Level 3 Subclass Feature from.
