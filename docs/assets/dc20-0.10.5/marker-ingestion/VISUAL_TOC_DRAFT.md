## Interpretation Rules

1. Large blue section titles become level 1 navigation groups.
2. Blue small-caps lines become article candidates.
3. Black indented lines become child headings within the nearest article.
4. Repeated headings such as `Starting Equipment`, `Level 1 Class Features`, and `Subclasses` are class-scoped, not global.
5. Page numbers are PDF printed page numbers from the visual table of contents.

## Review Notes

1. `Martial Chapter` and `Spellcaster Chapter` appear visually under `Combat Rules`, but they behave like major subchapters. Treat them as high-priority article groups.
2. Spell schools under `Spells` are article groups, while individual spell names in the body should become child atoms generated from content, not from this TOC.
3. Class pages need scoped IDs, for example `classes/cleric/starting-equipment`, not `starting-equipment`.
4. The visual TOC does not expose individual feature names like `Rage`; those should come from class section parsing after class grouping is settled.
5. The visual TOC gives the best source for high-level hierarchy. It should override Marker heading levels.
