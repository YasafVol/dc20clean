# Martial Chapter

Martial characters in DC20 don't merely fight - they wage war with precision, discipline, and skill that elevates them far beyond the capabilities of an ordinary sword-wielder. Their prowess is represented through a specialized combat system which includes:

- *Maneuvers:* Tactical feats of strength, speed, and finesse used to control the battlefield.
- *Enhancements:* Modifications to Maneuvers that add extra impact, reach, area effects, or debilitating conditions, or upgrades that empower Martial Attacks with increased damage, range, or area of effect.

<!-- source anchors: page-48-1 -->
<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/maneuvers -->
## Maneuvers

Maneuvers are the signature techniques of martial combatants; deliberate, high-impact actions that go beyond basic attacks. Whether it's a sweeping strike to knock enemies back or a sudden burst of speed to reposition, Maneuvers allow warriors to control the flow of battle with skill and force.

<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/maneuvers/maneuvers-known -->
### Maneuvers Known

Martial Classes (Barbarian, Champion, Commander, Hunter, Rogue, Monk, and Spellblade\*) know a number of Maneuvers as shown in the Maneuvers Known column of their Class Tables, plus any Maneuvers learned from Class Features, Talents, or Martial Path progression (see ["Character Paths" on page 192](#page-191-1) for more information).

<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/maneuvers/maneuver-resources -->
### Maneuver Resources

Each Maneuver has a minimum number of Action Points (AP) that must be spent to perform it. Many Maneuvers also include a minimum number of Stamina Points (SP) that must be spent to perform them. This is known as the Base SP Cost of a Maneuver.

#### Action Points

Performing any Maneuver requires spending Action Points (AP). Maneuvers that affect a single target typically cost **1 AP**, while those that affect multiple targets usually cost **2 AP**.

#### Stamina Points

Some Maneuvers have an SP cost that you must spend to perform them. The maximum amount of SP you can spend on a single Maneuver must be equal to or lower than your **Stamina Spend Limit** (see ["Combat Resources" on page](#page-40-1) [41](#page-40-1) for more information on Stamina Points).

*DC Tip: Remember, your Stamina Spend Limit is equal to your Combat Mastery.*

<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/maneuvers/maneuver-characteristics -->
### Maneuver Characteristics

#### Duration

Maneuvers can either be instantaneous or continuous in their duration, and some Maneuvers require you to Sustain them in order to keep them going (see the ["Sustained](#page-43-1) [Action" on page 44](#page-43-1) for more information).

- *Instantaneous:* Instantaneous Maneuvers take effect the moment they're performed.
- *Continuous:* These Maneuvers last a specified amount of time. They often end early when the affected creature Succeeds on a subsequent Save against the effect, it or another creature within 1 Space performs an Action to end the effect, or some other method stated to end the effect occurs (such as breaking line of sight).
- *Sustained:* Some effects require you to Sustain them or they end.

#### Range

This is the distance within which a target of the Maneuver, or the origin of its Area of Effect, must be. When selecting the targets of your Maneuver, they must be within the listed Range and fulfill any other listed requirement of the Maneuver (such as being visible or capable of hearing you). If the chosen Maneuver uses an Area of Effect to determine its targets, the origin of the Maneuver must be within its listed Range (see ["Areas of Effect" on page 157](#page-156-1) for more information).

<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/maneuvers/performing-a-maneuver -->
### Performing a Maneuver

In order to perform a Maneuver, you must know the Maneuver and spend the required amount of Resources (Action Points and Stamina Points). When you perform a Maneuver, you must make a Check (such as an Attack Check or Martial Check) to determine the Maneuver's degree of success or failure. Your Check is compared against the target's Defense (such as PD or AD), the Maneuver's DC, or the target's Contested Check.

<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/enhancements -->
## Enhancements

Enhancements amplify the impact of a character's offense, turning well-executed strikes into devastating blows. They can increase damage, extend range, affect wider areas, or inflict conditions, allowing martial characters to adapt to the shifting demands of battle.

<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/enhancements/maneuver-enhancements -->
### Maneuver Enhancements

Maneuver Enhancements allow you to spend additional AP or SP to increase the Maneuver's potency (such as damage, duration, range, number of targets, or Area of Effect). Maneuver Enhancements are specific to each Maneuver and are included in a Maneuver's description.

<!-- source anchors: page-49-0 -->
#### Performing a Maneuver

#### Enhancement

When you perform a Maneuver, you can spend additional SP to add a Maneuver Enhancement with the Maneuver. If the Maneuver requires a Check (such as an Attack Check or Martial Check), you must choose to add the Maneuver Enhancement before you make the Check. You can perform the same Maneuver Enhancement multiple times per Maneuver, unless stated otherwise.

<!-- source anchors: page-49-1 -->
<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/enhancements/martial-enhancements -->
### Martial Enhancements

Martial Enhancements empower a character's Martial Attacks with added force and versatility. By spending Action Points, a combatant can strike harder, reach farther, or break a foe's focus. These enhancements allow martial characters to elevate even their simplest strikes into potent tools of war, adapting on the fly to pressure, positioning, or opportunity. Some Attack Enhancements require a certain type of Weapon to perform (see ["Equipment" on page 164](#page-163-1) for more information).

**Example:** *When you make an Attack with an Axe against 3 creatures using the* **Whirlwind Maneuver***, you can spend* **1 AP** *to add the* **Martial Enhancement: Damage** *and* **1 AP** *to add the* **Attack Enhancement: Bleeding** *available only to Axes. 1 target of your choice takes* **+1** *damage from the Attack and another target of your choice (can be the same target) must make a Save against your Save DC to avoid* **Bleeding***.*

#### Performing a Martial Enhancement

When you make a Martial Attack, you can spend 1 additional AP or SP to add a Martial Enhancement to the Attack. You must choose to add the Martial Enhancement before you make the Attack Check. You can perform the same Enhancement multiple times per Attack, unless stated otherwise.

*DC Tip: Don't forget anyone can spend an additional* **1 AP** *to gain ADV on their Attack Check.*

#### Martial Enhancements

All characters with Weapon Combat Training gain access to the following Martial Enhancements:

- *Damage:* The Attack deals **+1** damage against 1 target of the Attack.
- *Range:* The Attack's Range increase by 1 Space if it's a Melee Attack, or by 5 Spaces if it's a Ranged Attack. This Enhancement can only be performed once per attack.
- **Daze:** A creature of your choice targeted by the Attack makes a Might Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target becomes **Dazed** until the end of their next turn.

*DC Tip: Remember, a* **Dazed** *creature can't Sustain any effects at the start of their turn! While Martial characters can't stop a Spell from being cast like a Spellcaster can with a Spell Duel, a Martial Character can more easily disrupt a Sustained Spell by causing the Spellcaster to lose focus.*

**Example:** *You spend* **2 AP** *and* **1 SP** *on an Attack against an enemy creature.* **1 AP** *to take the* **Attack Action** *(make 1 Attack Check),* **1 AP** *to add the* **Martial Enhancement: Damage** *to deal* **+1** *damage, and* **1 SP** *to add the* **Martial Enhancement: Range** *to grant your Attack some extra range.*

<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/categorizing-maneuvers -->
## Categorizing Maneuvers

<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/categorizing-maneuvers/maneuver-types -->
### Maneuver Types

There are several types of Maneuvers that allow you to produce a variety of powerful effects.

#### Attack

Focused on delivering powerful, aggressive strikes, Attack Maneuvers deal damage, and are the core of a martial character's offensive toolkit—optimized for impact, precision, or area control.

#### Defense

Defense Maneuvers reinforce durability and survival. Whether by parrying incoming blows, bracing against force, or redirecting attacks, these Maneuvers let martial characters hold the line and protect themselves or allies under pressure.

#### Grapple

Grapple Maneuvers specialize in close-quarters control: pinning, restraining, or throwing enemies. These Maneuvers allow a combatant to dominate a single opponent by controlling their movement and actions.

#### Utility

Utility Maneuvers offer battlefield versatility beyond direct combat. They may include repositioning, movement tricks, environmental interaction, intimidation, or setting up allies for success. These Maneuvers shift the flow of battle through clever use of space and timing.

## Advanced Maneuvers

<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/advanced-maneuvers/combo-maneuvers -->
### Combo Maneuvers

*Prerequisite*: Martial Path

During a Combo Maneuver, the creature performing the SP Effect is referred to as the **Performer** while the other creatures assisting are called **Participants**.

*Trigger*: You can see an SP Effect you know being performed, provided the SP Effect's **Performer** or a target is within 2 Space of you.

*Reaction*: You can spend an amount of AP equal to the base AP cost of the SP Effect you are trying to assist. When you do, the SP Effect gains a **+2** bonus to its Check and Save DC for this turn.

*Spending SP*: You can choose to spend 1 or more SP to add SP Enhancements to the **Performer's** SP Effect (up to your Stamina Spend Limit).

**Example:** *Martials A & B each have a Stamina Spend Limit of 3. Martial A spends* **1 AP** *and* **3 SP** *to perform the Bash Maneuver. Martial B can also spend* **1 AP** *and* **3 SP** *as a Reaction to increase the total SP spent on the Maneuver to 6 (the Stamina Spend Limit of both Martials together). Martial A then gains a* **+2** *bonus to their Martial Attack.*

*DC Tip: Martials must be within 2 Spaces of each other when performing a Combo Maneuver but they can spend Stamina Points up to each of their Stamina Spend Limits together. However, Spellcasters performing a Combo Spellcasting can do so from a range of 5 Spaces but they can only spend a total amount of Mana Points equal to only the Caster's Mana Spend Limit.*

## Maneuvers

<!-- rulebook-id: combat-rules/martial-chapter/maneuvers-2/attack-maneuvers -->
### Attack Maneuvers

Focused on delivering powerful, aggressive strikes, Attack Maneuvers deal damage, and are the core of a martial character's offensive toolkit—optimized for impact, precision, or area control.

#### Heroic Bash

The force of your Attack pushes the target away.

- *Cost:* 1 AP + 1 SP
- *Range:* Weapon or Unarmed Strike

Make a Melee Martial Attack against the PD of a target within range, and it makes a Might Save against your Save DC. **Hit:** The target takes the damage of your Weapon or Unarmed Strike. **Save Failure:** The target is pushed 1 Space away from you. **Save Failure (each 5):** The target is pushed up to 1 additional Space.

*Knock Prone*: After the result, you can choose to reduce the total distance a target is pushed by 1 Space to knock them **Prone** instead.

*Airborne*: When you choose to push them upward into the air, the distance they're pushed is halved.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Increased Distance*: (X SP) The distance pushed increases by X Spaces.

*Bowling Ball*: (1 SP) If the target collides with another creature as part of this movement, the creature it collides with must make a Might Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target is knocked **Prone**. **Save Failure (each 5):** The target is also pushed 1 Space away from you.

#### Savage Strike

You cause a creature to bleed with the ferocity and precision of your attack.

- *Cost:* 1 AP + 1 SP
- *Range:* Weapon or Unarmed Strike

Make a Martial Attack against the PD of a target within range, and it makes a Repeated Physical Save against your Save DC. **Hit:** The target takes the damage of your Weapon or Unarmed Strike. **Save Failure:** The target becomes **Impaired** or begins **Bleeding** (your choice) for 1 minute.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Improved Bleed*: (X SP) On a failed Save, the target gains **Bleeding X** for the duration.

*Improved Impaired*: (X SP) On a failed Save, the target gains **Impaired X** for the duration.

*Weakened*: (2 SP) On a failed Save, the target is **Weakened** for the duration.

#### Sunder Strike

You make a target vulnerable to Physical damage.

- *Cost:* 1 AP + 2 SP
- *Range:* Weapon or Unarmed Strike

Make a Martial Attack against the PD of a target within range, and it makes a Physical Save against your Save DC. **Hit:** The target takes the damage of your Weapon or Unarmed Strike. **Save Failure:** The target gains Physical Vulnerability (1) for 1 Round.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Improved Vulnerability*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The Vulnerability increases by 1.

*Break DR*: (1 SP) On a failed Save, the target doesn't benefit from PDR for the duration.

*Break Resistance*: (2 SP) On a failed Save, the target doesn't benefit from Physical Resistance for the duration.

#### Swift Strike

You move around the area, attacking 1 creature you pass along the way.

- *Cost:* 1 AP + 1 SP
- *Range:* Weapon or Unarmed Strike

You move a number of Spaces up to your Speed and make a Melee Martial Attack against the PD of 1 target within your Melee Range at any point during this movement. **Hit:** The target takes the damage of your Weapon or Unarmed Strike.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Disengage*: (1 SP) This movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks.

*Subsequent Strike*: (1 AP + 1 SP, Repeatable) You gain additional movement equal to half your Speed and you can target an additional creature using the same Attack Check.

#### Meteor Strike

A mid-air strike.

- *Cost:* 1 AP + 1 SP
- *Range:* Weapon or Unarmed Strike

Your Jump Distance increases by 2, you Jump up to your Jump Distance without spending movement (provided you can move), and make a Melee Martial Attack against a target within range at any point during your jump or where you land.

*Reaction*: You can spend an additional **1 SP** to perform this Maneuver as a Reaction to a creature entering a Space within your Jump Distance that's at least 1 Space above the ground.

*DC Tip: Remember, your Long Jump Distance equals your Jump Distance in Spaces, and your High Jump distance equals your Jump Distance in feet.*

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Falling Strike*: (1 SP) The creature makes a Physical Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The creature falls **Prone**.

*Slam Down*: (1 SP, Requires Falling Strike) On a failed Save, the target doesn't benefit from any ability that prevents it from falling as a result of being knocked **Prone** in midair until the end of the turn.

*Impact Crater*: (1 AP + 1 SP) All creatures within a 1 Space aura of where you land must make a Physical Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The creature falls **Prone**.

*Larger Crater*: (2 SP, Repeatable, Requires Impact Crater) The size of the Aura increases by 1.

#### Cleave

You swing wide to attack targets around you.

- *Cost:* 2 AP
- *Range:* 2 Space Arc (Self)

Make an Area Martial Attack against the AD of every target within range. **Hit:** The target takes the damage of your Weapon or Unarmed Strike.

*Reach Weapon*: If you have a Reach Weapon, you can extend the Point of Origin 1 Space away from you. The Arc must still be facing away from you.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Damage*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The damage increases by 1.

*Area*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The Arc increases by 1 Space.

*Careful Cleave*: (X SP) You can choose X targets in the area to avoid with the Attack.

*Sweep*: (2 SP) Each target makes an Agility Save. **Save**

**Failure:** The target is knocked **Prone**.

#### Whirlwind

You attack in a circle around you, attacking everyone within range.

- *Cost:* 2 AP
- *Range:* 1 Space Aura

Make an Area Martial Attack using a Melee Weapon or Unarmed Strike against the AD of every target within range. **Hit:** The target takes the damage of your Weapon or Unarmed Strike.

*Reach Weapon*: If you have a Reach Weapon, you can choose to increase the Aura by 1 Space. When you do, you can't Attack targets within 1 Space of you.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Damage*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

*Area*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The Aura increases by 1 Space.

*Vortex*: (2 SP, Repeatable) Each target makes a Might Save. **Save Failure:** The target is pushed up to 1 Space horizontally in a direction of your choice. The target is moved 1 additional Space each time you use this Enhancement.

#### Pathcarver

You attack every creature in a line.

- *Cost:* 2 AP
- *Range:* 4 Space Line (Self)

Make an Area Martial Attack with a Melee Weapon or Unarmed Strike against the AD of every target in a 4 Space Line. **Hit:** The target takes the damage of your Weapon or Unarmed Strike.

After Attacking, you can move to any Space within 1 Space of the area without provoking Opportunity Attacks from the targets and without spending your movement.

*Reach Weapon*: If you have a Reach Weapon, you can extend the Point of Origin 1 Space away from you. When you do, the Line must extend in a direction away from you.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Damage*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

*Increase Length*: (1 SP, Repeatable) The length of the Line increases by 2 Spaces.

*Carved Path*: (2 SP, Repeatable) Each target makes a Might Save. **Save Failure:** The target is pushed 1 Space horizontally out of the area in a direction of your choice. The target is moved 1 additional Space each time you use this Enhancement.

#### Piercing Shot

You fire a powerful shot that pierces through creatures in a line.

- *Cost:* 2 AP
- *Range:* Ranged Weapon or Weapon with the Toss or Thrown Property

Make an Area Martial Attack against the AD of every target within a 8 Space Line that's Point of Origin is within Range. The Line must extend away from you. **Hit:** The target takes the damage of your Weapon.

*Close Quarters Penalty*: You have DisADV on the Attack if you're within the Melee Range of at least 1 enemy, unless that enemy is **Incapacitated**.

*DC Tip: Remember, you have DisADV on any Attack beyond your Weapon's Normal Range.*

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Damage*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

*Area*: (1 SP, Repeatable) The length of the Line increases by 4 Spaces.

*Penetrating Shot*: (1 SP) The Attack ignores Cover but is blocked by 6 inches (15 cm) of wood or soft earth (dirt, mud, or sand), or 1 inch (25 mm) of metal or rock.

#### Scattershot

You unleash a cone of projectiles.

- *Cost:* 2 AP
- *Range:* Self
- *Requirements:* Ranged Weapon

Make an Area Martial Attack against the AD of every target within a 3 Space Cone. **Hit:** The target takes the damage of your Weapon.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Damage*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

*Area*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The length of the Cone increases by 1 Space.

*Stagger*: (1 SP) Each creature makes a Might Save. **Save Failure:** The target is unable to take Reactions for 1 Round.

#### Volley

You bombard an area with a series of projectiles.

- *Cost:* 2 AP
- *Range:* Ranged Weapon

Make an Area Martial Attack against the AD of every target within a 3 Space Diameter Sphere that's Point of Origin is within Range. **Hit:** The target takes the damage of your Weapon.

*Close Quarters Penalty*: You have DisADV on the Attack if you're within the Melee Range of at least 1 enemy, unless that enemy is **Incapacitated**.

*DC Tip: Remember you have DisADV on any Attack beyond your Normal Range and you can't attack beyond your Long Range.*

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Damage*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

*Area*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The diameter of the Sphere increases by 1 Space.

*Rain of Fire*: (1 SP) Targets within the area don't gain the benefits of 1/2 Cover against this Attack.

*Suppressive Fire*: (2 SP) Each creature makes a Charisma Save. **Save Failure:** The creature becomes **Hindered** for 1 Round

### Defense Maneuvers

Defense Maneuvers reinforce durability and survival. Whether by parrying incoming blows, bracing against force, or redirecting attacks, these Maneuvers let martial characters hold the line and protect themselves or allies under pressure.

*DC Tip: If you use a Maneuver as a Reaction to an Attack (such as Parry, Brace, and Evade), you must decide to use the Maneuver before the GM says the result of the Check.*

#### Parry

You deflect an attack.

- *Cost:* 1 AP
- *Range:* Self (or 1 Space if wielding a Melee Weapon or Shield)

*Trigger*: A target you can see within range (including yourself) is targeted by an Attack against its PD.

*Reaction*: You grant the target a **+5** bonus to its PD against this Attack.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Alert*: (1 SP) The Attacker's next Attack this turn against the target has DisADV.

*Disarm*: (1 SP) If the Attacker is within 1 Space of you or the target, they make a Physical Save. **Save Failure:** The target drops the Weapon used for the Attack to the ground once the Attack is resolved. **Save Failure (5):** You gain possession of the Weapon, provided you have a free hand to hold it.

#### Brace

You stand firm against an attack.

- *Cost:* 1 AP
- *Range:* Self (or 1 Space if wielding a Shield)

*Trigger*: A target you can see within range (including yourself) is targeted by an Attack against its AD.

*Reaction*: You grant the target a **+5** bonus to its AD against this Attack.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Targets*: (1 AP, Repeatable) You can target 1 additional creature within range that's also targeted by the same Attack.

*Grapple*: (1 SP) If the Attacker is within 1 Space of you, they make a Physical Save. **Save Failure:** The Attacker becomes Grappled by you once the Attack is resolved.

#### Side Step

You avoid an enemy attack by quick repositioning.

- *Cost:* 1 AP
- *Range:* Self

*Trigger*: You're targeted by an Attack.

*Reaction*: You impose DisADV on the Attack against you. You can move 1 Space after the Attack, provided you stay within range of the Attack.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Evasion*: (1 SP) You gain ADV on any Saves imposed by the Attack.

*Disengage*: (1 SP) Movement made as part of this Maneuver doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks.

*Slip Away*: (1 SP) You can move up to your Speed instead of 1 Space and you no longer need to stay in range of the Attack.

#### Protect

You take damage for a nearby ally.

- *Cost:* 1 AP
- *Range:* 1 Space

*Trigger*: A target you can see within range is Hit by an Attack.

*Reaction*: The damage of the Attack is shared between you and the target. The damage you take bypasses any Damage Reduction you have.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Targets*: (1 AP, Repeatable) You can target 1 additional creature within range that's also targeted by the same Attack.

*Taunt*: (1 SP) The attacker makes a Charisma Save. **Save Failure:** They're **Taunted** by you for 1 Round.

*Heroic Protect*: (2 SP) The Attack made against your target is made against you instead. You take all of the damage and effects instead of them, and if the Attack is accompanied by a Save (as with a **Dynamic Attack Save**), you make the Save instead of the target. If you were also originally a target of the Attack, you resolve both separately.

*Resistance*: (1 SP) You gain Resistance (Half) to any damage you take from using this Maneuver. The cost of this Enhancement increases by **1 SP** if you use the **Heroic Protect Enhancement**.

#### Endure

You push through an attack.

- *Cost:* 1 AP + 1 SP
- *Range:* Self (or 1 Space if wielding a Shield)

*Trigger*: A target you can see within range (including yourself) is Hit by an Attack.

*Reaction*: The target takes **1** less damage from the Attack.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Heroic Endure*: (X SP) The target takes X less damage from the Attack.

*Steadfast*: (1 SP) The target isn't Vulnerable to damage from the Attack.

*Repel*: (X SP) After the Attack, if the Attacker is within 1 Space of you or the target, they make a Might Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** They are pushed X Spaces away. **Save Failure (each 5):** +1 Space pushed.

#### Resolve

You take a moment to catch your breath and refocus.

• *Cost:* 1 AP + 1 SP

• *Range:* Self

Make a **DC 15** Martial Check. **Failure:** You gain **2** Temp HP for 1 Round. **Success:** You gain **3** Temp HP for 1 Round. **Success (each 5): +1** Temp HP.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Increased Temp HP*: (1 SP, Repeatable) You gain +**2** Temp HP.

*Physical Resolve*: (1 SP) You gain ADV on Physical Saves for the duration.

*Mental Resolve*: (1 SP) You gain ADV on Mental Saves for the duration.

#### Swap

You swap places with a nearby creature.

• *Cost:* 1 AP

• *Range:* 1 Space

You swap Spaces with a willing creature within range. If you or the target creature provoke Opportunity Attacks as a result of swapping Spaces, those Attacks are all made against you.

*Reaction*: You can spend an additional **1 SP** to perform this Maneuver as a Reaction in response to a creature within 1 Space being Attacked. When you do, you also impose DisADV on the Attack against them.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Escape*: (1 SP) The target can immediately move up to its Speed after swapping Spaces.

*Seamless Swap*:(1 SP) Swapping Spaces doesn't provoke any Opportunity Attacks.

*Hostile Swap*: (1 SP) You can target an unwilling creature. When you do, make a Martial Check against the target's Physical Save. **Check Success:** You swap Spaces with them.

### Grapple Maneuvers

Grapple Maneuvers specialize in close-quarters control: pinning, restraining, or throwing enemies. These Maneuvers allow a combatant to dominate a single opponent by controlling their movement and actions.

#### Slam

You slam a Grappled creature.

• *Cost:* 1 AP • *Range:* Melee

Make a Melee Martial Attack against the target's AD. **Hit:** The target takes **1** Bludgeoning damage.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Finishing Slam*: (1 SP) You deal 2 additional damage. The Grapple ends.

*Stunned*: (2 SP, Repeatable) The target makes a Physical Save. Save Failure: They are **Stunned 1** for 1 Round. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

*Wall Rake*: (X SP) You slam 1 **Grappled** creature into a wall within 1 Space and move up to X Spaces along the wall, ending your movement within 1 Space of the wall. The target makes a Repeated Might Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** They gain **Bleeding X** per Space moved.

#### Restrain

You restrain a grappled target.

• *Cost:* 1 AP + 1 SP

• *Range:* Melee

You make a Martial Check against the target's Repeated Physical Save. **Contest Success:** The target is **Restrained** until the Grapple ends.

*DC Tip: A creature can spend 1 AP to attempt to break a Grapple (see ["Grapple" on page 43](#page-42-1) for more information).*

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Prone*: (1 SP) The target also falls **Prone** (you don't fall **Prone** unless you choose to do so).

*Chokehold*: (2 SP) The target also can't speak or breathe until they are no longer **Restrained** by you.

*DC Tip: A creature that can't breathe begins holding their breath. In Combat, a creature's Breath Duration is equal to its Might (minimum of 1) in Rounds.*

#### Body Block

You reposition a grappled creature to shield yourself from damage.

• *Cost:* 1 AP + 1 SP

• *Range:* Melee

*Trigger*: A creature that's not Grappled by you Hits you with an Attack.

*Reaction*: Make a Martial Check against the Grappled target's Might Save. **Check Success:** The damage from the Attack is shared by you and the Grappled creature, and you can move the Grappled creature to any Space adjacent to you immediately afterwards.

*DC Tip: If the triggering Attack involved a Save (as with a Dynamic Attack Save) that moves you or your Grapple target out of your reach, you can still perform this Maneuver to resolve the damage (as if it the Hit happens moments before the forced movement).*

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Switch*: (1 SP) After you complete the Reaction, you switch places with the Grappled target. This movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks against either you or the target.

*Partial Block*: (1 SP) Your portion of the shared damage is reduced to 0.

*Full Block*: (2 SP) The Attack made against you is made against the Grappled creature instead. They take all of the damage and effects instead of you, and if the Attack is accompanied by a Save (as with a **Dynamic Attack Save**), they make the Save instead of the you. If they were also originally a target of the Attack, they resolve both separately.

#### Throw Creature

- *Cost:* Throw (1 AP)
- *Range:* Melee

### Utility Maneuvers

Utility Maneuvers offer battlefield versatility beyond direct combat. They may include repositioning, movement tricks, environmental interaction, intimidation, or setting up allies for success. These Maneuvers shift the flow of battle through clever use of space and timing.

#### Recover

You take a moment to catch your breath and refocus.

• *Cost:* 1 AP • *Range:* Self

You attempt to spend Rest Points to regain HP equal to the Rest Points spent. Make a **DC 15** Martial Check. **Failure:** You can spend 2 Rest Points. **Success:** You can spend up to 3 Rest Points. **Success (each 5):** You can spend 1 additional Rest Point.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Increased Healing*: (1 AP or 1 SP, Repeatable) You can spend 1 additional Rest Point.

*Repeat Save*: (1 AP or 1 SP) You can repeat a Save against an effect you're subjected to.

#### Heroic Pass Through

When you take the Pass Through Action, you can add the following Maneuver Enhancements. When you add a Maneuver Enhancement (or perform the Action using SP), you're considered to be performing a Maneuver.

*Stamina Action*: You can spend **SP** instead of **AP** to perform the **Pass Through** Action.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Brush Past*: (1 SP) The creature's Space isn't considered Difficult Terrain for this movement and you don't provoke an Opportunity Attack from them if you leave their reach as part of the same movement.

*Let's Go*: (1 SP) On a Success, you can pull a willing creature within 1 Space of you to an unoccupied Space within 1 Space of the creature you pass.

#### Line Run

You run through a line of enemies.

• *Cost:* 2 AP + 1 SP

• *Range:* Self

You move in a Line up to your Speed and make a Martial Check. Each creature within the Line must make a Physical Save against your Martial Check when you attempt to move through its Space. **Contest Failure:** You're unable to move through the creature's Space, preventing you from moving any further. **Contest Success:** You can move through the creature's Space and they are knocked **Prone**.

**Example:** *You make a Martial Check with a result of 15. If your Speed is 5, you can move up to 5 Spaces in a straight Line. If there are 3 creatures in that Line, your Martial Check of 15 would be compared to a Martial Check each creature makes as you attempt to pass through their Space, one by one. If the first creature in your path gets a 15 or higher, you can't pass through their Space and stop moving.*

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Distance*: (1 SP, Repeatable) You can move up to an additional half your Speed.

*Brush Past*: (1 SP) The Space of every creature who fails the contest isn't considered Difficult Terrain for this movement and you don't provoke an Opportunity Attack from them.

#### Reposition

Sprint across the battlefield

• *Cost:* 1 AP + 1 SP

• *Range:* Self

*Trigger*: A creature ends their turn.

*Reaction*: You immediately move up to your Speed.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Agile*: (1 SP) You ignore Difficult Terrain during this movement.

*Disengage*: (1 AP) You gain the benefits of the **Disengage** Action for the Movement. When you use this Enhancement, you can spend 1 additional SP to gain the benefits of the **Full Disengage** Action instead.

*Coordinated Movement*: (2 SP, Repeatable) A willing creature of your choice within 1 Space of your original position can move without spending its own movement, provided they're able to move and they end their movement within 1 Space of your destination.

#### Heroic Taunt

You perform a powerful taunt.

- *Cost:* Taunt Action (1 AP)
- *Range:* Taunt Action (10 Spaces)

When you take the **Taunt** Action, you can add the following Maneuver Enhancements. When you add a Maneuver Enhancement, you're considered to be performing a Maneuver.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Sustained*: (1 SP, Sustained) The duration increases to 1 minute but each creature makes a Repeated Save.

*Increased Range*: (1 SP) The range increases by 5 Spaces.

*Legendary Taunt*: (1 SP) Creatures other than you have Resistance (Half) to damage dealt by the **Taunted** targets, provided the target isn't also **Taunted** by those creatures.

*Mass Taunt*: (1 AP + 1 SP) You instead target each creature of your choice within 5 Spaces that can see or hear you. When you choose this Enhancement, the cost of all other Enhancements (except **Sustained**) is doubled.

#### Heroic Intimidate

You perform a powerful intimidate.

- *Cost:* Intimidate Action (1 AP)
- *Range:* Intimidate Action (10 Spaces)

When you take the **Intimidate** Action, you can add the following Maneuver Enhancements. When you add a Maneuver Enhancement, you're considered to be performing a Maneuver.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Sustained*: (1 SP, Sustained) The duration increases to 1 minute but each creature makes a Repeated Save.

*Increased Range*: (1 SP) The range increases by 5 Spaces.

*Frightened*: (1 SP) Targets are **Frightened** instead.

*Mass Intimidate*: (1 AP + 1 SP) You instead target each creature of your choice within 5 Spaces that can see or hear you. When you choose this Enhancement, the cost of all other Enhancements (except **Sustained**) is doubled.

#### Heroic Shove

You perform a powerful shove.

- *Cost:* Shove Action (1 AP)
- *Range:* Shove Action (1 Space)

When you take the **Shove** Action, you can add the following Maneuver Enhancements. When you add a Maneuver Enhancement, you are considered to be performing a Maneuver.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Increased Push*: (X SP) The distance pushed increases by X Spaces.

*Stand Up*: (1 SP) If you're **Prone**, you can stand up before or after making the Check without spending movement.

*Mass Shove*: (1 AP + 1 SP) You instead target each creature of your choice within a 1 Space Aura. When you choose this Enhancement, the cost of all other Enhancements (except **Stand Up**) is doubled.

#### Heroic Extend Jump

You perform a powerful jump.

- *Cost:* Extend Jump Action (1 AP)
- *Range:* Self

When you take the **Extend Jump** Action, you can add the following Maneuver Enhancements. When you add a Maneuver Enhancement (or perform the Action using SP), you're considered to be performing a Maneuver.

*Stamina Action*: You can spend **SP** instead of **AP** to perform the **Extend Jump** Action.

#### Maneuver Enhancements

*Instant Jump*: (1 SP) If performing a Standing Jump, your Jump Distances isn't halved.

*Increased Jump*: (1 SP, Repeatable) Your Jump Distance is increased by 2 for the Jump.

*Heroic Landing*: (1 SP, Repeatable) You reduce any damage from falling by 2 for the Jump.
