# Support DC20

If you want to help this project continue forward and give feedback on the direction of that growth, WHILE getting awesome rewards and exclusive DC20 content… the best way to do that is on Patreon. Even at the entry reward tier you get access to Patron only livestreams where I show the development of the game LIVE with y'all, and the support goes a long way toward what we can do each month! Higher reward tiers even get early access to DC20 content, GM Binders, extra content, and even playtest groups to play in (possibly with me as your GM).
