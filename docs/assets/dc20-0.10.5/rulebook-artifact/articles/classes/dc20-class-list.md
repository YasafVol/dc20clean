# DC20 Class List

<!-- source anchors: page-207-0 -->
In DC20, all characters belong to one of several **Classes**, each defining their role, strengths, and progression in combat and adventure. Classes fall into two primary categories: **Martial** and **Spellcaster**.

**Martial Classes** rely on physical prowess, tactical skill, and Stamina to overcome their foes—these include the **Barbarian**, **Champion**, **Commander**, **Monk**, **Hunter**, and **Rogue**.

**Spellcaster Classes**, on the other hand, wield magical power drawn from arcane, divine, or primal sources—such as the **Bard**, **Cleric**, **Druid**, **Sorcerer**, **Warlock**, and **Wizard**.

Standing at the crossroads of both worlds, the **Spellblade** blends martial discipline with spellcasting, making it both a Martial and a Spellcaster Class.

## Martial Classes

- Barbarian
- Champion
- Commander
- Hunter
- Monk
- Rogue
- Spellblade\*

## Spellcaster Classes

- Bard
- Cleric
- Druid
- Sorcerer
- Spellblade\*
- Warlock
- Wizard
