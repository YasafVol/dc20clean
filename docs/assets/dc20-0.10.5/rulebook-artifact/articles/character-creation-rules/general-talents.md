# General Talents

General Talents allow you to improve in a variety of ways, unrelated to Class.

## Ancestry Increase

*General Talent*

You gain 4 Ancestry Points.

## Attribute Increase

*General Talent*

You gain 2 Attribute Points to put into any Attribute of your choice.

*DC Tip: You can only increase an Attribute up to your Attribute Limit.*

## Skill Increase

*General Talent*

You gain 4 Skill Points

*DC Tip: You can convert Skill Points into Trade Points or Language Points. See Mastery & Training for more information.*

## Martial Expansion

*General Talent*

*You can only gain this Talent once.*

You gain the following benefits:

- *Combat Training:* You gain Combat Training with Weapons, Heavy Armors, and all Shields.
- *Stamina Regen:* You gain the Stamina Regen of the Class of your choice or the Spellcaster Path Stamina Regen (see ["Character Paths" on page 192\)](#page-191-1). You can only benefit from 1 Stamina Regen per Round.
- *Maneuvers Known:* You learn 2 Maneuvers.

## Spellcasting Expansion

*General Talent*

*You can only gain this Talent once.*

You gain the following benefits:

- *Combat Training:* You gain Combat Training with Spell Focuses.
- *Spell List:* You add 1 Spell Source or 3 Spell Schools of your choice to your Spell List.
- *Spells Known:* You learn 3 Spells from your Spell List.
