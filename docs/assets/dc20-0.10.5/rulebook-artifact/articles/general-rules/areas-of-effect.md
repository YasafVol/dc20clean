# Areas of Effect

<!-- source anchors: page-156-0 -->
An Area of Effect represents a zone of some kind that an effect covers, such as an **Arc**, **Aura**, **Cone**, **Line**, **Ring**, **Sphere**, or **Zone**.

*Point of Origin*: The Point of Origin for an Area of Effect is always 1 Space from which it extends (Arc, Cone, Line, or Wall) or is centered on (Aura, Ring, or Sphere). If the Area of Effect can't be centered on 1 Space then it's instead centered on the 4 most center Spaces.

**Example:** *On a Grid Map, a 3 Space diameter Sphere is 3 Spaces wide where the center of that Sphere is 1 Space. Alternatively, a 6 Space diameter Sphere is 6 Spaces wide where the center of that Sphere is the center most 4 Spaces. This is because there isn't only 1 Space that sits directly in its center.*

## Arc

An Arc forms a near half circle. An Arc's size is expressed as a radius in Spaces that extends in a 180° arc from the Point of Origin in a direction of your choice. The height of an Arc is 1 Space. An Arc's Point of Origin isn't included in its Area of Effect.

<!-- source anchors: page-156-1 -->
## Aura

An Aura's size specifies its radius in Spaces. An Aura extends in a straight line in all directions from a creature or object, but not through Full Cover, and moves with it for the duration. The source of the Aura chooses if the Point of Origin is subjected to the Aura's effects.

## Cone

A Cone extends 90° from its Point of Origin in a direction of your choice. A Cone's size specifies its length in Spaces. A Cone gets taller and wider as it gets longer. A Cone's Point of Origin isn't included in the its Area of Effect.

## Line

A Line's size specifies its length in Spaces. A Line is 1 Space tall and wide (unless stated otherwise) and extends from its Point of Origin in a direction of your choice. A Line's Point of Origin isn't included in its Area of Effect.

*Wall*: A Wall is a Line that extends vertically up or down in a specified number of Spaces.

## Ring

A Ring's size specifies its diameter in Spaces. A Ring extends horizontally in all directions horizontally from its Point of Origin, but not through Full Cover. A Ring's area is 1 Space high and only includes the Spaces at the edge of its diameter (not the interior). A Ring's Point of Origin isn't included in its Area of Effect.

*Ringwall*: A Ringwall is a Ring that extends vertically up or down in a specified number of Spaces from the edge of its diameter.

## Sphere

A Sphere's size specifies its diameter in Spaces. A Sphere extends horizontally in all directions from its Point of Origin, but not through Full Cover. A Sphere's Point of Origin is included in its Area of Effect.

*Cylinder*: A Cylinder is a Sphere that extends vertically up or down in a specified number of Spaces from the edge of its diameter.

*Dome*: A Dome is a hollow Sphere that's area only includes the Spaces at the edge of its curved surface (not the interior).

## Zone

A Zone is a series of Spaces connected to each other. Each Space within a Zone must be perpendicularly adjacent to at least 1 other Space of the Zone. A Zone's size specifies its total number of Spaces. A Zone extends from its Point of Origin in a direction of your choice. A Zone's Point of Origin isn't included in its Area of Effect.
