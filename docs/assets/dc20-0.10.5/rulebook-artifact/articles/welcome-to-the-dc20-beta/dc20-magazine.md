# DC20 Magazine

This is early access DC20 Content that might not be released for months or years to come, this is a HUGE way that you can playtest and get your hands on future content and see the direction of DC20. This is where future Classes, Subclasses, Monsters, Magic Items, Adventures, Rules, New Game Systems, and MORE will be showcased for the first time.

The DC20 Magazine is available at the entry level Reward Tier (\$5), and a new issue is released on the 1st of each month!
