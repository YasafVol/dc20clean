# Note from the Creator

First off, I just want to say thank you to anyone who has shown interest and supported DC20. It was scary to make the jump to publish my own game, and there were two different times where I almost had to pull the plug on the whole project... but it is YOU, this community, that has made all of this possible! Everything from watching the DC20 videos, buying the Alpha, supporting the Patreon, or now pre-ordering the project has brought this game to life!

I've been working on DC20 for years, and after the OGL situation I decided to release DC20 sooner than planned. I wanted to make a game system that was simple for a new player to pick up (even someone who had never played TTRPGs) but also had depth for more experienced players. I started out trying to make something more similar to 5e... but as the development process kept on, I found new rules and systems that I truly felt were better to build a foundation of a game around. DC20 has truly grown into its own unique standalone game, while maintaining the familiar feel of 5e. I wanted the rules to be intuitive and easy to remember, I wanted the combat to be engaging and dynamic with a focus around teamwork, and I wanted the game overall to feel FUN and EPIC! I truly feel like we did all of those things, and I can't wait to see the community's response!
