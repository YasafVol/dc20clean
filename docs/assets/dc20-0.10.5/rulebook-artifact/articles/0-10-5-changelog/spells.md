# Spells

- All spells have been changed to say "Tags" instead of "Spell Tags" for consistency. Many Spells have had Tags updated.
- Spell Source typos have been fixed
- Almost every single Spell received typo fixes, wording changes, quality of life changes, or balance changes.

Some Spells have been renamed (and possibly reworked):

- Summon Familiar has been renamed to Call Familiar
- Fly has been renamed to Blessing of Air
- Force Dome and Wall of Force have been reworked into Forcefield
- Vicious Mockery has been renamed to Mockery
- Toxic Aura has become Toxic Burst
- Close Wound renamed to Close Wounds
- Earth Blessing renamed to Blessing of Earth
- Gravity Sinkhole reworked into Gravity Well
- Absorb Element renamed to Absorb Elements

Some Spells have been removed or added to certain spell lists:

- Mass Heal removed from Primal list
- Radiant Beam removed
- Darkness removed from Primal list
- Wind Blessing removed
- Acid Rain removed
- Lightning Cloud removed
- Poison Cloud removed
